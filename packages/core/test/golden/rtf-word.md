# Why mixing works

Practising one kind of problem at a time feels smooth, but a test mixes them, and telling them apart is the skill. **Interleaving** the kinds of problem beats blocking them.

In Japanese the idea is 間隔反復 (spaced repetition), and its root word is 記憶.

[The original study](https://example.edu/interleaving)[^1] is in the appendix.

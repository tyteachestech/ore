### Notes on retrieval practice

Testing yourself beats rereading. A café study group that quizzes each other learns more than one that rereads its notes together — even when the rereading feels better.

Three habits help, and the **first** matters most:

- Space reviews out over days
- Mix topics in one session
- Explain each answer in your own words

*Write the question before you read the answer.* Then check it the next day: that’s the retrieval that sticks.

# Sweep 4b (iPhone): interruptions

Rules, driver and report: `docs/reviews/2026-11-13-codex-phone-edges/common.md` (read it first). Write under `codex-out/s4b/`. Use an ordinary seeded library; reseed with `start --fresh --lib` whenever a task needs things due again.

## Tasks, each twice
1. **No network.** The Simulator shares the Mac's network, so don't turn it off. Stand in for a dead network with links that can't answer: `http://10.255.255.1/` (hangs), `https://ore-test.invalid/page` (no such host), `https://httpbin.org/status/500`, `https://httpbin.org/delay/60`. Add each with +. Does it say what is happening, and how long until it gives up? Can he remove it, retry it, leave and come back? Nothing half-read may be saved as a source (D96).
2. **Imports cut off.** Add a real article (`https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Forgetting_curve`), a PDF (`https://arxiv.org/pdf/1706.03762`) and a podcast episode (any Apple Podcasts episode link); while each is coming in: go home and come back, quit with `launch`, and `reset`-free relaunch twice in a row. Does each finish, or say why not, with nothing duplicated? The Simulator has no speech model, so an episode can't be heard: check it says so plainly instead of waiting forever.
3. **Leaving mid-task.** Home and back, then `launch` (a quit), in the middle of: an Explain answer half typed in Review, a rename, a new skill's name, a note being written, a highlight's note, find in source with a word typed, the + sheet with a link pasted. What was typed should still be there, or at least nothing he finished is lost; say which.
4. **Rotation and the keyboard.** `rotate landscape` and back on Today, a source while reading (does he keep his place?), Review with the keyboard up, the drawer open, a menu open, a picture open full screen. Then with the keyboard up in every field you can find, can he see what he types and reach the button that saves it?
5. **Fast and wrong.** Double tap fast on Start review, Finish, a rating, Archive, Remove, + and Done; swipe two rows quickly one after another; a swipe left then undo then swipe again; links into the app that point at nothing: `xcrun simctl openurl $ORE_SIM_DEVICE "ore://open?id=nope"` and `"ore://open"` and `"ore://garbage"`.

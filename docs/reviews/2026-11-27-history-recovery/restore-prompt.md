# Your run: restore (core and the index after files go back in time)

Your name is `restore`. Restore three things the way an agent would, then read every view core and the CLI give of them:
1. **One source** archived, cut (D111, its cut record lives in gitignored `.ore/cuts/`) or deleted, then brought back from an older snapshot: its doc.md, meta, kept original, cards and highlights. Does `ore list`, `ore show`, search, the due queue, `ore card show --review` and `ore doctor` see it whole? Does a later `ore cut --undo` or Reimport on it do the right thing, or apply a cut record to text it no longer matches?
2. **One note** (`notes`, a diagram, a chat in `chats/`) overwritten, then restored with `git checkout <sha> -- <file>`: does the index (`.index/library.sqlite`) pick the old content up, given checkout gives the file a new mtime but the index may key on size or a hash? Backlinks (`links.ts`) to and from it?
3. **A whole day** rolled back with `git checkout <sha> -- .` (files added since stay) and with `git reset --hard <sha>` (they go). Append-only logs (`reviews/*.<device>.jsonl`, `sessions/`, `annotations.<device>.jsonl`, D78.4) shrink: do cached card states in the index, the FSRS replay (`scheduler.ts`, `queue.ts`), `ore stats`, the open-session marker in `.ore/`, the skills page numbers and the end-of-day receipts come out matching the logs, or does the index keep the later reviews? Cards whose source went, sources whose cards went, a skill in `skills.yaml` that went.
Also: the snapshot right after a restore (does it commit the restore cleanly, or fight a detached HEAD, an `index.lock`, a half-done merge, a stash?).

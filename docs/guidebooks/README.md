# Guidebooks

Four evidence-based guidebooks Tyler built (with AI research and verification pipelines) before and alongside Ore. The app's decisions and research briefs cite them. Each is a full book in Markdown and EPUB, plus the research it was written from.

| Guidebook | What it covers | Paper summaries |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [Learning Science](learning-science/Learning%20Science.md) | How memory works, retrieval, spacing, interleaving, elaboration, cognitive load, practice, self-regulation. Plus a one-page [design reference](learning-science/Learning-Science_Design_Reference.md) | 31 |
| [Teaching](teaching/Teaching.md) | Teaching through video and tutorials: the curse of knowledge, explanations that land, worked examples, motivation, guiding without a classroom | 40 |
| [Gamification](gamification/Gamification.md) | Self-determination theory, flow, points/badges/leaderboards, streaks and progress, novelty decay, anti-patterns | 42 |
| [UX and UI Design](ux-ui-design/UX%20and%20UI%20Design.md) | App design: information architecture, navigation, hierarchy, typography, color, feedback, forms, motion, defaults | 25 |

Each folder has:

- `<Title>.md` and `.epub`: the book. Every chapter ends with its evidence table and an "If you're designing a learning system" section.
- `research/paper-summaries/`: one structured summary per research paper (findings, effect sizes, limits, relevance), written while reading the full text.
- `research/`: the source inventories and DOI lists, and for gamification the three raw deep-research reports the book started from.

Not included: the paper PDFs themselves (copyrighted, about 250 MB; every summary names its DOI), and summaries of books, which may be added later.
